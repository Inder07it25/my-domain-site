Picvio

Picvio backs up photos from your iPhone to your own Google Photos account. You choose what to upload. SISdar Apps does not host or sell your photo library.

Privacy Policy · Terms · Support

What Picvio does

  • Link one or more Google accounts for backup destinations.
  • Organize uploads by year or month into Google Photos albums you control.
  • Optional Premium features for more accounts, quality options, and background uploads.

Google account access

When you tap Add account, Picvio asks Google for only the permissions needed to upload selected photos and manage media the app created in your Google Photos library. Tokens stay on your device for the actions you request. You can disconnect in the app and revoke access in your Google Account permissions at any time.

Picvio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
