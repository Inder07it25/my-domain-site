Privacy Policy

SISdar Apps is committed to protecting your privacy while delivering apps, games, and this website. This policy explains how data is collected and used. Published by SISdar Apps and developed by Sweta Prakash.

Publisher and developer

Published by SISdar Apps. Developed by Sweta Prakash, Founder, CEO and CTO. Contact: sisdarapps@gmail.com.

This website

The public marketing pages do not use an account system. If you use the contact form, your name, email, topic, and message stay in your email app until you send them to us. We do not run a server-side form database. The admin catalog tool is for SISdar staff only and is not intended for public use.

Data collected in apps

  • Anonymous analytics
  • Device information
  • Crash reports
  • Advertising identifiers

Third-party services

Apps and games may use the following services to support the experience:

  • Google AdMob
  • Firebase Analytics

How data is used

No personal information is sold. Data is used to improve gameplay and app functionality, diagnose crashes, measure anonymous usage, and, where ads are shown, serve and measure advertising.

Children’s privacy

Our apps and this website are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, email sisdarapps@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Your rights and deletion requests

You may request access to or deletion of personal information we hold about you, including advertising identifiers tied to support requests, by emailing sisdarapps@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 48 hours.

Jurisdiction

SISdar Apps operates from India. These practices are intended to comply with applicable Indian law and with the privacy rules of the Apple App Store and Google Play.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top of this page will change when we do. Continued use of the website or apps after an update means you accept the revised policy.
